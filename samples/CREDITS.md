# Sample credits

Free pictures to try the scenes that use your own images, and the test clip for the hand tracker.

- `01-antonello-da-messina.jpg`: *Portrait of a Young Man*, Antonello da Messina, ca. 1470.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435581
- `05-giovanni-di-francesco.jpg`: *Portrait of a Woman*, Giovanni di Francesco del Cervelliera, ca. 1445.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437859
- `07-adriaen-hanneman.jpg`: *Portrait of a Woman*, Adriaen Hanneman, ca. 1653.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436630
- `08-velazquez.jpg`: *Portrait of a Man, Possibly a Self-Portrait*, Velázquez, ca. 1635.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437874
- `11-hugo-van-der-goes.jpg`: *Portrait of a Man*, Hugo van der Goes, ca. 1475.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436521

  The five portraits are public-domain paintings from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access program (CC0),
  scaled down to 1000 px high.

- `hands.mp4`: the first 3 seconds of "A Woman Dancing Gracefully" by **Pavel Danilyuk**, from Pexels
  (https://www.pexels.com/video/a-woman-dancing-gracefully-6939968/), cropped to her upper body and scaled to
  640×480, no sound. Free to use under the Pexels licence (https://www.pexels.com/license/). `npm run smoke` plays
  it as a fake webcam to test hand tracking.
